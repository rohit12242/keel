# Triage queue — findings that are real but out of scope

A review finding that is correct and **outside the intent being built** lands here.
It does not become an extra step in the running plan: that is how a sprint grows by
a quarter while nobody decides it should.

Each row is either promoted to its own intent at the next planning, or dropped with
a reason. Dropped rows stay, with the reason — a queue that only grows is a backlog,
and a queue that forgets is a promise nobody kept.
